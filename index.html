LubeLogger mobile

A native Android companion for your self-hosted LubeLogger server — log fuel, track service and repairs, and stay on top of maintenance reminders for every vehicle, right from your phone.

View source on Codeberg Privacy policy
Requires your own LubeLogger server. LubeLogger mobile is a companion app — it talks only to the self-hosted LubeLogger instance you point it at, using an API key you generate. It has no cloud service of its own and will not work without a server. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to LubeLogger or Hargata Softworks.

What it does

🚗

Your whole garage

Manage every vehicle in one place — add, edit and remove vehicles, see their photos, and open a dedicated screen for each one with distance in km, miles or engine hours.

⛽

Fuel & mileage

Log fill-ups and odometer readings and watch your fuel economy, with average, min, max, distance and cost. Add a fuel or odometer entry straight from a home-screen shortcut.

🔧

Complete history

Service, repairs, upgrades, tax, supplies, the planner and notes — every record type LubeLogger supports, all fully editable from the app.

🔔

Reminders

Stay ahead of maintenance with LubeLogger's reminders, plus optional background notifications that alert you when something is due or overdue.

📊

Dashboard & charts

A per-vehicle overview with fuel-economy trends, mileage by month, reminders by urgency and cost summaries — your data at a glance.

🎨

Made to fit you

Attach receipts and documents to records, show, hide and reorder the record tabs, switch between metric and imperial units, and pick a light or dark theme.

Privacy & open source

LubeLogger mobile has no analytics, no advertising and no cloud service. Your API key is stored in encrypted, Keystore-backed storage on your device and is only ever sent to your own server. Read the full privacy policy.
